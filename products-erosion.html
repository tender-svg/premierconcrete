Erosion Control

Shoretec® offers the most durable, effective and environmentally-friendly erosion control revetment methods for fighting severe erosion problems. Our systems resist displacement from high water velocities and wave loads, won’t degrade over their design life, and blend into the landscape as native grasses grow through the open cells — at 20–50% lower cost than alternative erosion control methods.

Shoretec® ACB Systems

Shoretec® articulated concrete block (ACB) systems are durable, aesthetic and environmentally compatible, at a cost savings of up to 50% when compared to traditional hard armor methods. ACBs are an aesthetic and functional alternative to rip-rap, poured-in-place concrete and other heavy duty erosion protection systems. Shoreblock® BD, SD and Shoreloc® Hand-Placed Block Series have been successfully tested by Colorado State University in accordance with the FHWA hydraulic performance testing protocol (FHWA-RD-89-199).

Applications
  • Dam overtopping
  • Auxiliary spillways
  • Levees
  • Diversion berms

The most advanced, flexible, permeable and versatile system in the erosion control industry. Shoretec® ACB Systems

A flexible, interlocking matrix of concrete blocks of uniform size, shape and weight connected by a series of cables which pass longitudinally through preformed ducts in each block. Shoreblock® SD combines the porosity, flexibility and vegetation encouragement of lightweight blankets with the non-erodible, self-weight and high tractive force resistance of a rigid lining. Mats are available in 8′ width and lengths up to 40′, and can be joined to achieve greater lengths. Cables are sized to provide a 5 to 1 cable strength to mat weight ratio; open cells comprise about 20% of the mat area.

ShoreFlex®

A vegetated concrete block erosion control mat. ShoreFlex® eliminates unsightly rip rap and non-uniform poured in place concrete. It is a permanent erosion prevention system that can be installed to shield channel side slopes and beds, pipe and culvert inlets/outlets, shoreline, and almost any place with hydraulic erosion protection needs. Every ShoreFlex® mat is a high strength geogrid cast into 5,000 psi concrete blocks (6.5″ × 6.5″ × 2.5″) spaced 1.5″ apart, with approximately 30% open area for vegetation. Four models are available, differing by geogrid and backing.

Applications
  • Channel side slopes & beds
  • Pipe & culvert inlets/outlets
  • Shoreline
  • Roadside
  • Canal & ditch lining
  • Landfills
  • Let-downs
  • Boat ramps

ShoreJax™

ShoreJax™ are flexible interlocking concrete armor units available in a variety of sizes, used to resist the erosive forces generated due to high velocity water. They can be placed in random configurations (such as those required to line underwater scour holes) or in a uniform pattern for improved performance in wave attack or energy dissipation applications. The voids in the system can be filled with desired soils and vegetation for an aesthetically pleasing result.

Applications
  • Energy dissipation
  • Stream bank protection
  • Bridge pier scour protection
  • Toe stabilization
  • Drop structures
  • Weirs
  • Velocity reduction at pipe outfalls
  • Habitat development
  • Water flow control

EPEC System™

EPEC is a patented multi-layer revetment system used to reinforce and protect a surface exposed to possible high water velocities, wave attack and/or potential hydraulic jump from super critical flows. The system includes both a cellular confinement layer (BaseLok® GeoCell) and an articulated block layer (Shoreblock®), deployed together to provide enhanced protection of ground surfaces.

Applications
  • Dam overtopping
  • Auxiliary spillways
  • Levees
  • Diversion berms

EPEC System™

A cellular confinement layer placed on the ground surface: a matrix of open cells with rigid walls designed to be filled with aggregate or sand. Its 3D load transfer platform in the stone drainage layer improves system performance and resistance against hydraulic jump.
