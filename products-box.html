Box Culverts

Precast box sections are recommended for installations where circular or elliptical concrete pipe cannot provide adequate flow capacity, and as a superior substitute for cast‐in‐place box culverts, long span metal arches, short bridges, and multi‐barrel circular culverts or drains.

Reinforced Concrete Box Culvert (RCBC)

Precast monolithic box sections in spans of 3–14 ft with a variety of rises, in 8 ft lengths. Tongue and groove joints with a glued-on neoprene gasket.

Applications
  • Storm drains
  • Highway bridges
  • Utility tunnels
  • Highway culverts
  • Railroad culverts
  • Short span highway bridges
  • Groundwater recharge systems
  • Jacked or tunneled installations
  • Underground stormwater retention structures
  • Livestock, pedestrian or golf cart undercrossing
  • Replacement of existing open channels or ditches
  • Highway and road crossings
  • Stormwater conveyance

Open Bottom Reinforced Concrete Box Culvert (Open Bottom RCBC)

Three-sided precast box sections set on footings, leaving the natural channel bottom in place for crossings where the existing streambed must be preserved.

Applications
  • Stream and creek crossings
  • Fish and wildlife passage
  • Natural channel bottoms
  • Low-water crossings
  • Pedestrian and trail underpasses

Custom Box Culverts

Box sections fabricated to non-standard spans, rises, skews or loading requirements for projects that standard sizes do not fit.

Applications
