Masonry

Premier Concrete manufactures concrete masonry units (CMU) for commercial, residential and industrial construction across Louisiana and the Gulf South. Our block is produced to the latest ASTM C90 standard in 4″, 6″, 8″ and 12″ sizes, in lightweight and normal weight mixes, with standard, bullnose, lintel, bond beam and split face shapes. Concrete block gives builders fire resistance, sound control, thermal mass and long-term durability against moisture, pests and severe weather. Premier’s Dry Block water repellent is available in every masonry unit and is tested to withstand wind-driven rain up to 80 mph. Lightweight CMUs contribute to sustainability by increasing durability, improving energy performance and reducing life cycle costs, which is why architects and contractors often choose lightweight block for its quality and economy. Our team helps you select the right unit, mix and finish for each project, and we deliver from local stock so your job stays on schedule.

4″ Units

4″ concrete masonry units manufactured to the latest ASTM C90 standard with compressive strength exceeding 2,000 psi. Architectural Masonry

Architectural masonry is decorative-faced concrete block that combines the strength of a structural unit with the look of a finished facade. Units are available in a range of textures, aggregates and integral colors to suit the design intent. Split face units are cast as two joined blocks and split apart, producing a rough face that resembles hand-hewn stone, so every piece is slightly different. Premier can produce custom shapes, sizes and colors, and our team works with architects to match samples and finishes before production.

Applications
  • Decorative facades
  • Exterior walls
  • Commercial buildings
  • Screen & site walls

Mobile Home Block

Mobile home blocking provides the stability and support a manufactured home needs to stay level and protected against wind, soil movement and moisture. Premier Concrete Products manufactures high-strength concrete masonry units for every part of a mobile home pier, from the footing pad at the base to the solid cap and leveling blocks under the frame. Installed to the guidelines set by local building codes and the home manufacturer, our block helps homeowners, dealers and installers keep mobile homes structurally sound for years to come. Our normal weight units are the preferred choice for mobile home leveling and placement.

Applications
  • Mobile and manufactured home foundations
  • Piers and supports
  • Leveling and blocking
  • Skirting support
