Superior protection for onshore and offshore pipelines, servicing the entire Gulf Coast. OS Mats are articulating wet cast concrete mats over 4,000 psi, and filled sand bags are available on short notice.

OS Mats

The OS Mat system is an articulating mat consisting of wet cast concrete units manufactured to over 4,000 psi compressive strength. OS Mats are cast with an integral corrosion resistant, UV stabilized synthetic cable providing long-term durability, and Premier Concrete’s TigerTuff™ non-abrasive coating withstands multi-handling for placement.

Applications
  • Pipeline protection
  • Erosion & scour protection
  • Pipeline cover & separation
  • Natural gas pipelines
  • Buried and unburied flowlines
  • Pipeline and cable crossings
  • Water supply pipes
  • Pipeline valve protection
  • Thermal expansion
  • Reusable offshore foundation

Sand Bags

Filling large quantities of sand bags can be a logistical nightmare: sourcing bags, trucking, arranging for bulk sand, finding a dry place to fill and securing labor. With prepositioned truckload inventory, we can supply filled sand bags on short notice — over 10,000 filled daily. Each pallet is poly covered and wrapped to protect the sand from getting wet.

Applications
  • Flood control and levee repair
  • Pipeline support and padding
  • Erosion control
  • Emergency response
  • Ballast and weighting

3:1 Bags

3:1 Sand-Cement blended product is a 4000 PSI mix packaged in a burlap bag constructed from jute, a natural fabric that is biodegradable. When stacked and interlocked, the bags mold together to form a strong cementitious matrix once the burlap material degrades from weathering. The finished look is that of stacked riprap or stones.

Applications
  • Pipeline support and padding
  • Scour repair
  • Erosion control
  • Headwall and outfall repair
  • Offshore stabilization
