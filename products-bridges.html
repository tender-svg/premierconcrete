Precast Bridges

Premier Concrete manufactures precast bridge components that help owners and contractors deliver accelerated bridge construction at lower cost. Our deck panels, bent caps and approach slabs are cast in the controlled environment of our plant using fabricated forms, giving each element improved geometry and more accurate dimensions. Because components are built off-site, site preparation and pile driving can proceed while superstructures and bent caps are fabricated, so elements arrive on time, road closures are shorter and traffic disruptions and user costs are kept to a minimum. Louisiana has approximately 13,000 bridges and one of the highest bridge densities in the nation, with nearly 20 bridges per 100 miles of public roadway. About 50% are owned by parishes and municipalities, and many are short-span structures. Premier Concrete supplies these owners and their contractors with precast bridge systems that are easier to construct, shorten project duration and reduce overall cost.

Bridge Deck Panels

Precast deck panels reduce field forming and cast-in-place concrete on bridge decks. Premier Concrete produces full-depth panels for complete short-span decks and partial-depth panels that work with girder-supported spans.

Applications
  • Short-span road bridges
  • Bridge deck replacement
  • Private and access road crossings
  • Agricultural crossings
  • Rural and parish road bridges
  • Short-span creek crossings

Bridge Deck Panels

Short-span bridges often utilize longitudinal, full-depth deck panels that are joined to form the complete bridge width and span length. These bridges may be surfaced with asphalt; however, many designs do not require an asphalt overlay. In many cases, the bridge railing is cast integrally with the exterior panel. Alternatively, depending on the contractor's preference, the railing may be cast on-site.

Bridge Deck Panels

Partial-depth deck panels consist of precast concrete segments supported by girders. These panels not only reduce the amount of cast-in-place concrete required but also serve as stay-in-place forms for the cast-in-place deck topping. Many contractors prefer partial-depth deck panels because they improve worker safety and provide economical construction.

Bent Caps

Precast bent caps minimize the need for field forming of both large and small cap sections. Caps are cast at our plant and delivered directly to the construction site, ready to set on their supporting structures.

Applications
  • Pile bent bridges
  • Bridge widening and replacement
  • Short-span highway and parish road bridges
  • Accelerated bridge construction
  • Railroad and pedestrian bridges

Bent Caps
