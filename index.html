Our story

Manufacturing concrete products since 1996

Premier Concrete Products began with a plant in Houston, Texas and an administrative office in Louisiana. Today our production is centered in Denham Springs, where a large library of molds produces masonry units, retaining wall systems, pavers, erosion control mats and culverts.
