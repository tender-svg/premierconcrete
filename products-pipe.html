Pipe Culverts

Reinforced concrete pipe (RCP) is the preferred choice for storm water systems across the country. With a proven history of reliability, it’s clear why municipalities and contractors rely on concrete pipe to build resilient infrastructure. RCP is designed and manufactured to withstand a complete range of underground environments and runoff profiles. Concrete pipe serves as both a conduit and a structure, characteristic that sets it apart from flexible materials. With compressive strengths typically from 4,000 to 8,000 psi, it will not burn, rust, tear, buckle or deflect, and offers a service life of 100 years plus.

Reinforced Concrete Pipe Round (RCP)

Round reinforced concrete pipe for storm drains, culverts, outfalls and utility tunnels. It is designed to withstand a complete range of underground environments and runoff profiles, serving as both a conduit and a structure.

Applications
  • Culverts
  • Outfalls
  • Utility tunnels
  • Storm drains
  • Low pressure sewer force mains
  • Treatment plant piping
  • Low pressure water supply systems
  • Impound overflow conduits
  • Irrigation distribution systems
  • Groundwater recharge systems
  • Cattle pass tunneling
  • Highway and road culverts
  • Subdivision drainage
  • Sanitary sewers

Reinforced Concrete Pipe Arch (RCPA)

Reinforced concrete arch pipe is a versatile solution for situations where overhead space limitations or depth constraints require an alternative to traditional round concrete pipes. It is produced with tongue and groove joints for sealing, using materials like mastic joint compound, preformed flexible bitumen, butyl rubber gaskets, mortar, or external joint wraps.

Applications
  • Low-cover road crossings
  • Culverts with limited headroom
  • Storm drains
  • Stream crossings
  • Drainage under driveways and access roads
